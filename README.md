# Da goated nav software

One easy, simple, Blahaj themed compass that doesn't care if you have data or not and works pretty much anywhere.

<img width="1365" height="597" alt="image" src="https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/bfbdc9e0-4555-42b3-a881-fea1bcfb5c5b" />

Now, in this day and age, we have some ... tools.

But they require so much data! Like my god are we being for real right now you want *4 megabytes of data* just to *open*????\
<small>that was sarcasm by the way</small>

Imagine how much data I'd be down by the time you decided to go anywhere.

Case in point, right now, on my phone, the most amount of data used by any one app is 79.8 MB by Google Maps. And I don't even use it that much !?

This was a simple 1 hour trip. Fair, I guess. But it can really add up, especially on smaller dataplans.

And so, introducing!

## Normal Maps!

Normal Maps only uses data when you are selecting your target location, and so it can be done on Wifi and this can run without any data at all! Now, of course, there may be a slight draw back of navigating by compass, but in reallity it isn't even that bad. All you really need to know is the general direction 99% of the time to take one of the best routes!

<img width="537" height="551" alt="image" src="https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/4118620e-9522-4a42-889f-a7c7f95fe3d2" />

A great example is the above in downtown Toronto, from our base at Startruptive to the nearest Ikea!

And, just like that, for a fraction of the data and, arguably, the more aura way, you are able to navigate around the big 6. And anywhere else!
